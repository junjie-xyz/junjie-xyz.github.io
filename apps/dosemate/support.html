常见问题

提醒需要联网吗？

不需要。提醒由 iOS 在本机安排和发送。请在系统设置中允许通知；提醒的实际显示方式仍可能受专注模式、系统通知设置和设备状态影响。

为什么没有收到提醒？

请依次检查药物是否启用、提醒时间是否正确、系统通知权限是否开启，以及专注模式是否允许相关通知。你也可以在应用设置中发送测试提醒。

数据保存在哪里？

药物和服药记录保存在设备上的应用沙盒中，不会自动上传到开发者服务器或 CloudKit。你可以主动生成 PDF、CSV 或 JSON 文件，并通过系统分享表选择保存位置。

如何删除数据？

你可以删除单条记录、完整删除药物及其历史，或在应用设置中删除全部数据。删除前请确认不再需要这些记录。

医疗免责声明

本应用仅用于服药提醒和记录，不提供诊断、治疗、药物选择、药物相互作用检查或剂量建议，也不能替代专业医疗意见。请严格按照医生、药师或药品标签的指示用药。紧急情况请立即联系当地急救服务。

Frequently asked questions

Do reminders require an internet connection?

No. iOS schedules and delivers reminders locally. Notification permission must be enabled. Delivery presentation may still be affected by Focus, system notification settings, and device state.

Why did I not receive a reminder?

Check that the medication is active, its reminder time is correct, notification permission is enabled, and Focus allows the notification. You can also send a test reminder from the app's Settings screen.

Where is my data stored?

Medication and dose records remain in the app's sandbox on your device. They are not automatically uploaded to a developer server or CloudKit. You can choose to generate PDF, CSV, or JSON files and save them through the system share sheet.

How can I delete my data?

You can delete an individual record, permanently delete a medication and its history, or delete all data from the app's Settings screen. Confirm that you no longer need the records before deleting them.

Medical disclaimer

This app is for medication reminders and record keeping only. It does not provide diagnoses, treatment, medication selection, drug interaction checks, or dosage advice and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Follow the instructions of your doctor, pharmacist, or medication label. In an emergency, contact local emergency services immediately.
