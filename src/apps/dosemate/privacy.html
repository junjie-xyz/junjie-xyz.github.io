隐私政策

处理的数据

本应用处理用户输入的药物名称、剂量信息、服药计划、提醒偏好和服药记录。

存储与收集

药物数据仅存储在本机的 iOS 应用沙盒中。开发者不运营账户系统或应用服务器，也不会接收这些数据。本应用不会将个人健康信息存入 CloudKit 或应用控制的 iCloud 容器。

本应用不含广告、追踪、第三方分析或第三方运行时 SDK。

通知

通知权限为可选权限。授权后，iOS 会发送本机安排的服药提醒，提醒无需网络连接。锁屏提醒默认隐藏药物名称。用户可在系统设置中更改通知权限，并可在应用设置中更改锁屏显示方式。

导出

用户可在设备上生成 PDF、CSV 或 JSON 文件。只有用户主动操作时才会导出。用户通过系统分享表选择目标位置，其中可能包括“文件”、iCloud Drive、通信工具或其他已安装服务。导出文件可能包含敏感健康信息，分享后不再受本应用控制。

保留与删除

数据会保留在设备上，直到用户编辑数据、删除药物或记录、在设置中删除全部数据，或移除本应用。用户确认完整删除药物后，其关联历史也会被删除。

医疗用途

本应用仅用于服药提醒和记录，不提供诊断、治疗、药物选择、药物相互作用检查或剂量建议，也不能替代专业医疗意见。请严格按照医生、药师或药品标签的指示用药。紧急情况请立即联系当地急救服务。

English

Privacy Policy

Data processed

The app processes medication names, dosage details, schedules, reminder preferences, and dose records entered by the user.

Storage and collection

Medication data is stored only in the app's local iOS sandbox. The developer does not operate an account system or application server and does not receive this data. The app does not store personal health information in CloudKit or an app-controlled iCloud container.

The app contains no advertising, tracking, third-party analytics, or third-party runtime SDK.

Notifications

Notification permission is optional. If allowed, iOS delivers locally scheduled medication reminders. Reminders do not require a network connection. Lock-screen details hide medication names by default. The user can change notification permission and lock-screen behavior in Settings.

Export

The user may generate PDF, CSV, or JSON files on the device. Export occurs only after the user initiates it. The selected system share destination is controlled by the user and may include Files, iCloud Drive, messaging, or another installed service. Exported files contain sensitive health information and are no longer controlled by the app after sharing.

Retention and deletion

Data remains on the device until the user edits it, deletes a medication or record, deletes all data in Settings, or removes the app. Deleting a medication also deletes its associated history after confirmation.

Medical use

This app is for medication reminders and record keeping only. It does not provide diagnoses, treatment, medication selection, drug interaction checks, or dosage advice and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Follow the instructions of your doctor, pharmacist, or medication label. In an emergency, contact local emergency services immediately.
